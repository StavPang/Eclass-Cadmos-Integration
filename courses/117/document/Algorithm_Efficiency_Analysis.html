Algorithm Efficiency Analysis

Students will assess and compare the efficiency of different algorithms through handson exercises focusing on time and space complexity Groups will present their findings in a collaborative online presentation fostering discussion and diverse perspectives

Learning Goals:

  • Assess the efficiency of different algorithms in terms of their time and space complexity

Activity Details:

Activity Type: Evaluating
Target Audience: Group
Facilitator: Teacher
Facilitator Role: The Teacher supports the learners by providing resources on algorithm efficiency guiding the analysis process and encouraging constructive discussion during presentations
